# A/B Testing
A/B tests randomly assign users to variants and compare metrics to estimate causal lift.
Power analysis sets sample size from baseline rate, MDE, α, and desired power. Peeking inflates false positives — use sequential methods or fixed horizons.
Guardrail metrics catch regressions (latency, revenue). CUPED and stratification reduce variance.
Simpson's paradox and novelty effects require careful segmentation and duration.
