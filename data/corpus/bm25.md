# BM25 Lexical Retrieval
BM25 is a probabilistic ranking function scoring documents by term frequency with saturation and document-length normalization, plus IDF weighting.
It excels at exact keyword matches and rare terms. It struggles with synonyms and paraphrases.
Hybrid retrieval combines BM25 with dense embeddings (e.g., reciprocal rank fusion) for robustness.
Tunable parameters k1 (TF saturation) and b (length normalization) default near 1.2 and 0.75.
