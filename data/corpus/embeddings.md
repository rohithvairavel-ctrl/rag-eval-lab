# Vector Embeddings
Embeddings map discrete objects (words, documents, users) into dense continuous vectors where similarity reflects semantic relatedness.
Word2Vec, GloVe, and contextual models (BERT) produce token embeddings. Sentence-transformers fine-tune for semantic similarity.
Cosine similarity and dot product are common retrieval metrics. Embedding quality drives RAG retrieval performance.
Dimensionality, training objective, and domain mismatch all affect downstream retrieval and clustering.
