# Overfitting and Underfitting
Overfitting occurs when a model fits training data too closely, including noise, and fails on unseen data. Signs include large train-test gaps and unstable coefficients.
Underfitting occurs when a model is too simple to capture the signal. Both train and test error stay high.
Mitigations for overfitting: regularization (L1/L2), dropout, early stopping, more training data, feature selection, and simpler architectures.
Mitigations for underfitting: richer features, deeper models, fewer constraints, and longer training.
