# Fairness and Bias in ML
Models can amplify historical bias in data. Measure disparate impact, equalized odds, and calibration across groups.
Mitigations: better data collection, reweighting, adversarial debiasing, and constrained optimization.
Transparency, documentation (model cards), and human oversight matter for high-stakes domains.
Fairness definitions can conflict — choose metrics aligned with stakeholder values and legal requirements.
