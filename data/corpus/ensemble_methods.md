# Ensemble Methods
Bagging (Random Forest) averages diverse trees trained on bootstrap samples to reduce variance.
Boosting (AdaBoost, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM) sequentially fits models to residual errors, reducing bias.
Stacking trains a meta-learner on base-model predictions. Ensembles often win tabular competitions.
Tradeoffs: better accuracy vs interpretability, training time, and inference latency.
