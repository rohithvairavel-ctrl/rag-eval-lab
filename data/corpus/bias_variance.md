# Bias-Variance Tradeoff
Bias measures how far a model's average prediction is from the true value. High bias means underfitting — the model is too simple to capture patterns.
Variance measures how much predictions change across different training sets. High variance means overfitting — the model memorizes noise.
Total expected error decomposes into bias² + variance + irreducible noise. Regularization, more data, and model complexity control the tradeoff.
Cross-validation helps estimate generalization error and choose a sweet spot between under- and overfitting.
