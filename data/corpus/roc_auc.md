# ROC Curve and AUC
The ROC curve plots true positive rate (recall) against false positive rate across classification thresholds.
AUC (area under the ROC curve) summarizes ranking quality: probability that a random positive ranks above a random negative.
AUC is threshold-independent and robust to class imbalance for ranking, but does not reflect calibration.
PR-AUC (precision-recall AUC) is often more informative when positives are rare.
