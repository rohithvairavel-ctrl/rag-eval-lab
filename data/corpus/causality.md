# Causal Inference Basics
Correlation is not causation. Causal questions need assumptions: exchangeability, positivity, consistency (or DAG-encoded structure).
Randomized experiments are gold standard. Observational methods: matching, inverse propensity weighting, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables.
Confounders bias naive associations; colliders induce spurious associations when conditioned on.
Uplift models estimate heterogeneous treatment effects for targeting.
